God of War – Egypt Fanmade

A third-person combat prototype deeply inspired by God of War, set in a hand-crafted Egyptian mythological world.

Overview

A fully original combat prototype built from the ground up, channeling the visceral, cinematic identity of God of War — reimagined in an Egyptian mythological setting. The core design goal was to engineer a system where every swing, dodge, and impact feels intentional, weighty, and deeply satisfying. This project serves as a technical showcase of advanced combat architecture.

My Role

Full Stack Developer & Combat Designer: Sole developer responsible for all aspects of the prototype — from engine architecture to final game feel polish.

Combat Systems: Designed and implemented the complete melee combat loop, including combo chains, input buffering, hit detection, and enemy reaction logic.

Animation Engineering: Integrated and rigged animation state machines for both player and enemies using Unity Animator with smooth blend trees and transition control.

Game Feel & Feedback: Applied advanced "juice" techniques — hit stops, camera shakes, screen vignettes on damage, directional sound design, and dynamic particle impact VFX to reinforce satisfying hits.

Character Controller: Architected a responsive, physics-aware third-person character controller with precise dodge rolls and movement acceleration curves.

Tech Stack

Unity, C#, FSM (Animator), Cinemachine, New Input System, Physics Layers, Custom Hit Detection, VFX Graph, Post Processing (URP).

Problem & Solution

Problem: Achieving the signature "weighty" combat feel of AAA titles like God of War is notoriously difficult — early builds felt too responsive and floaty, stripping the sense of impact from every action.

Solution: Implemented a layered feedback system: frame-precise hit-stop freezes on enemy contact, physics-based knockback with custom deceleration curves, carefully tuned animation exit times, and a predictive input buffer system that lets players queue their next attack during an animation — resulting in punchy, readable, and deeply satisfying combat.
